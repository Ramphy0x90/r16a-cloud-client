Privacy Policy

Domovoi · Last updated {{ lastUpdated }}

Domovoi is a personal file and photo cloud, available as a website (domovoi.cloud, also reachable at cloud.r16a.cloud) and as mobile apps for Android and iOS. This policy explains what personal data Domovoi processes, why, for how long, who else is involved, and what rights you have. It applies to the website, the apps and the servers behind them.

2. Who can use Domovoi

Accounts are by invitation only; there is no public sign-up. Sign-in is handled by Domovoi's own identity service (auth.r16a.cloud), which the operator also runs. Domovoi is not directed at children.

3. What data is processed

Account information

From the identity service, each time you sign in: your username, email address, display name and an internal account identifier. Domovoi also stores your role and your settings (light/dark theme, default file view) and when your account was created and last changed.

Your files and photos

Everything you upload, and information about it: file and folder names, descriptions, sizes, folder structure, upload and change dates, who you share it with, and whether it is shared. Files are stored exactly as you upload them, so any information embedded in them stays there — for example a photo's date, camera details and, if your camera recorded it, its location. Domovoi reads the date a photo or video was taken to build your photo timeline; it does not otherwise analyse that embedded information. To show previews, Domovoi generates thumbnails, small blurred placeholders and, for some formats, converted copies of your images and videos.

Sharing

To let you share files, Domovoi shows signed-in users a list of other users (username, display name and email address) to pick from. People you share a file with can see it, and your display name as its owner. Files others share with you appear in your account until they stop sharing them.

Activity log for synchronisation

When files are added, changed or deleted, Domovoi records a short entry (which file, its name and folder, what happened and when) so your other devices can update. These entries are deleted after 90 days.

Technical data

Like any online service, the servers receive your device's IP address and basic request details (such as the address requested and the time) when you use Domovoi. These appear at most in short-lived server logs. Domovoi also keeps technical monitoring data about the servers (for example load and error rates), which is not designed to contain personal data.

What Domovoi does not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party crash reporting, no selling or renting of data, and no profiling. The mobile apps do not access your contacts or your location.

4. Why, and on what legal basis

Your data is used only to provide Domovoi to you:

  • to create and run your account and sign you in;
  • to store, show, synchronise, share and let you download your files and photos;
  • to keep the service secure and working (security, error analysis, backups).

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal basis is the performance of the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for security and operational logs and backups, the legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f)). Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), data is processed for these stated purposes only and limited to what they require.

5. Who else is involved

Domovoi's servers, storage and identity service are run by the operator personally, on equipment in Switzerland. One outside provider is involved:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) — traffic to domovoi.cloud passes through Cloudflare's network, which protects the site and forwards requests to Domovoi's servers. Because Cloudflare decrypts and re-encrypts this traffic, it processes your IP address and the content of requests (including files you upload or download) while in transit; it does not store your files. The mobile apps, the cloud.r16a.cloud address and sign-in (auth.r16a.cloud) connect to Domovoi's servers directly and do not pass through Cloudflare.

Cloudflare may process data outside Switzerland and the EU, including in the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US and Swiss–US Data Privacy Frameworks and offers the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, which are the safeguards relied on for these transfers.

Your data is not otherwise disclosed to anyone, except where required by law (for example a binding order from a Swiss authority).

6. How long data is kept

  • Account, files and photos: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Synchronisation activity log: 90 days.
  • Server logs: only briefly — they are rotated automatically, typically within a few days.
  • Technical monitoring data: about one month.
  • Backups: one month. Data you delete may remain in backups until they are rotated out, at most one month later.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time under Profile → Delete account, in the app or on the website. This permanently erases your account, your files and photos, their previews, your activity log and your shares; files other people shared with you stay with them. Your sign-in identity is then removed by the operator; you can also request that by email. Copies in backups disappear within one month.

8. Data stored on your device

To work quickly and offline, the apps and the website keep some data on your device: your sign-in tokens (in the device's secure storage on mobile), a temporary copy of folder listings (file names and details), thumbnails, temporary copies of files you open, and the files you choose to download. Signing out or deleting your account clears the listings, thumbnails and temporary copies; files you saved to your device (for example to the Downloads folder) stay until you remove them. On Android, the system's backup feature may include some of the app's stored data in your own device backup. The website uses your browser's storage only for signing in and for this temporary data — no tracking cookies.

The mobile apps ask for these permissions, only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — to take a photo to upload.
  • Photos — to choose photos and videos to upload.
  • Notifications — to show download progress.
  • Storage (older Android versions only) — to save downloads to your Downloads folder.

9. Security

All connections use encryption in transit (HTTPS). Access to each file is checked on every request, and only you and the people you share it with can reach it. Files are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on servers controlled by the operator, who could technically access them, and will only do so where needed to run the service, fix problems or comply with the law. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure; if a security incident affects your data, you will be informed as required by law.

10. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data and information about how it is processed;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted — you can do this yourself with Delete account;
  • receive your files in a portable form — you can download them at any time;
  • object to or request restriction of certain processing.

To exercise these rights, write to the email address in section 1. You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, edoeb.admin.ch); in the EU, the authority of the country where you live.
